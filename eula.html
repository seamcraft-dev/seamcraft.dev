5. Data and privacy

5.1 Your data stays on your machine. Seamcraft talks directly from your computer to Microsoft's APIs. Everything it keeps (sets, mappings, saved definitions and backups, cached metadata, recent connections and logs) is stored locally under your Windows user profile. None of it is sent to us. Our privacy policy describes this in full.

5.2 No telemetry. Seamcraft does not collect usage data, analytics or crash reports. The only connection it makes other than to Microsoft is its update check, which asks GitHub whether a newer version of Seamcraft is available; you can turn off the start-up check in Settings. If this changes, we will describe what is collected and let you turn it off before it is enabled.

5.3 Credentials. Seamcraft never stores a service principal's client secret; you enter it each time you connect. Interactive sign-ins are handled by Microsoft's sign-in libraries, through Seamcraft's own Microsoft Entra app, published by Common Data Solutions LLC. Your organization may require an administrator to approve that app before you can sign in. Seamcraft's logs record actions and errors and are designed not to contain secrets.
