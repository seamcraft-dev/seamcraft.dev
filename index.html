Seamcraft

Every piece of your Fabric, seamed together.

Seamcraft is a Windows app for Microsoft Fabric developers and administrators. It keeps Dev, Test and Prod consistent, moves pipelines and notebooks with the right connections, and fixes items in place without losing their IDs or schedules.

Latest version · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · No admin rights needed · Updates itself · All releases

The installer is signed by Common Data Solutions LLC. Windows may still say “Windows protected your PC” for a newly released version, until enough people have downloaded it. If it does, choose More info, check that the publisher is Common Data Solutions LLC, then choose Run anyway.

Getting started

  1. Install. Run the installer. It installs for your Windows user only and opens Seamcraft when it's done.
  2. Connect. Choose Connect and sign in with your work account, the one you use for Microsoft Fabric. The first time, Microsoft asks you to approve Seamcraft. If your organization only lets admins approve apps, the sign-in screen says so, and the in-app help (F1) has a link for your admin.
  3. Explore. Browse your workspaces like SQL Server Management Studio's Object Explorer. Nothing in Fabric changes until you choose an action that changes it.

What it does

Your whole Fabric estate at a glance

Tenants, workspaces, folders, items, connections and service principals in one tree, with several tenants open side by side.

Keep environments consistent

Group Dev, Test and Prod into sets, then compare them. A correctly mapped connection doesn't count as a difference.

Copy with references re-pointed

Copy pipelines and notebooks between workspaces and tenants, with every connection, workspace and item reference mapped to the target.

Update one workspace from another

Bring Test up to date from Prod, or any direction, for a whole workspace or folder in one pass.

Fix connections in place

Repair a pipeline's or a whole folder's connections without recreating anything, with a local backup and one-click restore.

Service principal ownership

Hand pipelines over to a service principal, so they don't break when the person who built them leaves.

Your data stays on your machine.

Seamcraft talks directly to the Fabric APIs from your desktop. Nothing is stored anywhere except the changes you choose to make in Fabric. See the privacy policy.
